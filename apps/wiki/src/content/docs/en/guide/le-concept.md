## Why this wiki?

abwiki is my **online second brain**. It holds everything I've learned and
everything I know, organized as simple, **searchable** Markdown notes.

The goal is simple: stop asking an AI chatbot or a search engine for information I
already know. If it's in my head, it's also in the wiki.

## The golden rule

1. **I search here first.**
2. If I don't find what I need, I search online.
3. I learn the answer, then I add it to the wiki.

That's how the wiki grows: it expands exactly with my knowledge.

## Structure

The wiki is organized by category — see the [homepage](/en/). Content is written
mainly in French; the English pages are for the interface and guides.
